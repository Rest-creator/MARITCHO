# Payments App

## Overview
The Payments app handles all financial transactions, escrow, and ledger operations for the Maricho platform.

## Responsibilities
- **Ledger Management**: Maintaining an immutable transaction history for deposits, balances, and releases.
- **Multi-currency Support**: Handling transactions across multiple currencies to accommodate local market conditions.
- **Cash Settlements & Escrow**: Managing deposits, releasing funds upon job completion, and tracking offline or cash settlements.
- **Payment Gateways**: Processing electronic payments via local gateways (e.g., EcoCash).
