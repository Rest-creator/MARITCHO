# Suburbs App

## Overview
The Suburbs app manages geographical data, service areas, and location-based logic for the platform.

## Responsibilities
- **Location Management**: Defining and managing suburbs, zones, and logistical boundaries.
- **Service Areas**: Mapping workers and crews to their operational areas based on travel means.
- **Geo-grounding**: Providing compound geo-grounding for accurate location tracking (like landmarks, narratives, and GPS pins), allowing precise job location capture.
