# Jobs App

## Overview
The Jobs app is the core operational engine for handling service requests, matching, and job execution workflows.

## Responsibilities
- **Job Intake**: Capturing job requests and requirements.
- **Quoting & Booking**: Managing the lifecycle from job quoting (with labor and materials breakdowns) to booking confirmation.
- **Execution Tracking**: Tracking job statuses, evidence collection (e.g., completion photos), and final sign-off.
- **Matching**: Connecting job requests to the most appropriate workers or crews based on skills, standing, and availability.
