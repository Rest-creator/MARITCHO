01 · The product

What Maricho is

Maricho is a hiring platform for skilled manual work. A household describes a problem and gets a verified tradesman at the door the same day. An employer describes a crew and gets one on site by Monday. And every completed job writes a permanent, evidenced entry into the worker's record.

01

A way to be hired

Workers publish what they can do — by voice, in their own language — set the areas they cover, and receive work that fits. No CV, no certificate, no office to walk into.

02

A way to hire

Buyers describe the problem, not the job title. Maricho returns three suitable people with their standing, their distance and a price band — and holds the money until the work is signed off.

03

A record that lasts

Every job produces dated photographs, a named client's confirmation and a rating. It belongs to the worker, travels with him, and is the reason his rate can rise.

The three problems it solves

ProblemHow it shows upWhat Maricho does about it
Skill is invisibleA man has fitted two hundred geysers and can prove none of it, so a paper certificate he doesn't have outranks work he has done.Issues evidence. Photographs, dates, a named client's sign-off, and an earned grade that rises with the work.
Trust is unverifiableA buyer cannot tell a good tradesman from a confident one until the work is half done and half paid.Verifies identity before anyone is bookable, publishes standing, inspects before quoting, and guarantees a redo.
Payment is unenforceable"Come back on Friday." Fare spent, day lost, no leverage.Holds a deposit from the moment of booking and releases the balance on sign-off.
The shape of the business

Two kinds of buyer walk through two different doors into the same pool of verified workers. Quick Hire serves households and small businesses: one job, near me, soon. Crew Hire serves organisations: a named number of workers, a role, a date range, a site. A worker verified once is hireable through either.

02 · Design principles

Seven rules that settle arguments

Every unresolved design question in this document is decided by one of these. They are written to be usable — each says what to do, not what to believe.

01

Evidence beats assertion

Nothing in a profile is a claim. If it cannot be photographed, dated, or confirmed by a named person, it does not appear. This applies to skills, to job history, and to standing.

02

The person decides; the system explains

Maricho narrows and reasons. A human always chooses. Any shortlist, ranking or rejection the product produces must be accompanied by the reason for it, in words the affected person can read.

03

Design for the worker's worst day

The worker is the party with the least power and the most to lose. Where a decision is finely balanced — defaults, notifications, penalties, dispute handling — it resolves in his favour and we absorb the cost.

04

Money moves before trust exists

Nobody travels on a promise. A booking is not real until a deposit is held, and the worker is told so before he spends a cent on transport.

05

Location is a service area, never an address

Workers publish where they will travel to. The product shows suburbs and distance bands. A worker's home never appears on a map, and a job address is revealed only when both sides have accepted.

06

Speak first, type only if you want to

Every input that matters accepts a voice note in Ndebele, Shona or English. Typing is an option, never a requirement. A product that requires literacy excludes precisely the people it exists to serve.

07

Assume no power, no signal, no data

The phone is cheap, the bundle is small and the electricity is intermittent. The product works offline, syncs later, and never spends a megabyte the user did not ask it to spend.

04 · Service scope

Six trades, one city

A hiring platform only works where it is dense. Six trades in Bulawayo, deep enough that any request gets three good answers within the hour, is worth more than eighty trades that each return one distant maybe.

A trade enters at launch only if it scores on all four tests: how urgently the buyer needs it, whether the ticket is large enough to carry a fee, whether the result can be photographed, and whether existing local supply is deep enough to fill demand from day one.

TradeUrgencyTicketPhotographableRole at launch
PlumbingVery highMediumYesThe emergency trade. A burst pipe cannot wait for a referral, which makes it the cheapest demand to win.
ElectricalHighMediumPartlySafety-critical, so verification is worth real money to the buyer and grades mean something immediately.
Solar install & serviceMediumHighYesThe growth trade. Intermittent grid power has made this the fastest-expanding skilled work in the country, with tickets large enough to carry the business.
Welding & fabricationLowMediumYesThe supply trade. Deep existing skill in the townships and visually striking output that makes records compelling.
Building, tiling & plasteringLowHighYesThe bridge trade. Multi-day jobs that lead naturally into crews and crew leads.
Appliance & refrigeration repairHighLow–mediumYesThe frequency trade. Small, repeatable jobs that teach the matching engine quickly and cheaply.

Two service models, because two kinds of work exist

Dispatch

One job, one worker, one visit

The six launch trades. A problem appears, a person is matched, the work is done, the record is written. Priced per job against a published band.

Recurring

A standing arrangement

Domestic and care work. Matched once, then supported: a fixed schedule, automatic payment, a rate that only changes by agreement, and a record of unbroken service. Enters once dispatch is stable, because it needs a different product, not a different category.

05 · What the product knows

Seven objects

The whole system is these seven things and the relationships between them. Get these right and every screen becomes obvious.

A record entry is immutable

Once a client signs off, the entry cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including Maricho. Corrections are appended, never overwritten. A record that can be quietly rewritten is not evidence.

Standing is computed, never assigned

No manual boosts, no paid placement, no favourites. Standing is a function of records and nothing else, and the function is published to workers in plain language.

06 · Structure

Three surfaces

One system, three ways in. Each is designed around what that person opens it to do, and nothing else.

WhatsApp is a full channel, not a notification pipe

A worker can run his entire working life through WhatsApp without opening the web app: receive a job, accept it, say he is on his way, send completion photographs, confirm he was paid. The web app is richer — his record, his money, his settings — but nothing essential is locked inside it. Buyers can post a job and approve a quote the same way.

07 · The job

From a leaking ceiling to a line in a record

One job, twelve states, three parties. The money sits in the middle the whole way, and every failure branch is a designed outcome rather than an edge case.

When it goes wrong

Buyer cancels late

Deposit goes to the worker. He was ready; his day was held.

Worker doesn't arrive

Deposit refunded, standing marked, a standby offered within the hour.

Scope grows on site

A revised quote must be approved in-app before work continues. Unapproved extra work is never expected of anyone.

Buyer is unhappy

Money freezes, both sides give their account, and the default remedy is a free return visit rather than a refund.

SCREEN 08 · WORKER

When there's a disagreement

The screen that decides whether this product deserves the trust of the people it is built for. It is designed to the third principle without compromise.

  • He answers before anything happens to him. No suspension, no ranking penalty, no mark on the record until a person has read both accounts.
  • His own job photographs are attached automatically. He is not asked to prove his case from memory.
  • Speaking is enough. A written-only appeal silently excludes the least literate workers, who are exactly the ones most likely to need one.
  • The default outcome is a return visit, not a refund. The buyer gets a working geyser, the worker keeps his day's pay, and the record shows a resolved matter rather than a failure.
09 · Standing

How a stranger becomes a known quantity

Trust is not a badge, it is a sequence. A worker climbs it by working; the platform's exposure to him rises as he does. The first two rungs are reachable without a single review, which is how anyone gets a first job at all.

Ratings run both ways

Buyers rate workers. Workers rate buyers — on paying, on scope, on how they were treated. A buyer with a pattern of unpaid extras stops being offered people.

Answer before consequence

A single complaint never removes anyone. Standing is frozen, not damaged, while a person reads both accounts and decides.

Everyone gets seen

A fixed share of every day's shortlists goes to identified workers with little history, and a cooldown stops the same five people taking everything.

Address discipline

A job address appears when both sides accept. A worker's home appears nowhere, to nobody, at any point.

Both people are safe

In-app help for either party, next of kin on file, and a number a person answers. A woman entering a stranger's property is protected as carefully as the household she enters.

The guarantee

Work that isn't right is put right at no extra cost. It is what a buyer is really paying the fee for, and it is why she chooses us over asking a neighbour.

10 · Money

How value moves

Holding

A booking is real when a deposit is held. The deposit is sized to cover travel plus a meaningful share of a day's earnings, so cancelling late costs something and travelling is never a gamble. The balance is committed when the quote is approved and released on the day the buyer signs off — same day, to EcoCash or InnBucks, with a receipt in the record.

Pricing

Maricho publishes a band for every job type, computed from completed comparable jobs in that suburb. The worker prices within the band; his grade lifts its ceiling. Neither party negotiates blind, nobody can be pushed beneath the floor, and a worker who improves earns more without having to argue for it. Above a set value, the quote is issued after the worker has inspected the job in person — never from a photograph.

Who pays

Quick Hire

A percentage of the job, charged to the buyer and disclosed before booking. It buys the verification, the held money and the guarantee.

Crew Hire

A placement fee or a monthly subscription, invoiced to the organisation. Fill rate and attendance records are what renews it.

Never the worker

Nothing to register, nothing to apply, nothing to be shortlisted. A platform that charges the people with the least money selects for desperation, not skill.

To confirm with a payments partner

Holding buyers' funds in Zimbabwe will most likely run on a licensed rail — a mobile money merchant arrangement or a bank partner — rather than Maricho holding balances directly. This conversation should happen before the flow is finalised, because the answer shapes it.

11 · Matching

How three names get chosen

Matching is the least visible part of the product and the one that most determines whether it feels fair. Four inputs, one hard rule, and an explanation attached to every result.

01

Can they do it

Meaning is matched, not keywords. "Someone to sort out a leaking geyser" should reach plumbers and refrigeration technicians alike, because that is who can actually help.

02

Can they get there

Declared service area first, distance second. How he travels matters more than how many kilometres — a man with a bicycle and a man with a bakkie do not have the same eight kilometres.

03

Are they free

Real availability, not an optimistic setting. Someone already on a job in another suburb is not a match, however good.

04

Have they done it before

Standing and relevant record entries. Weighted, never decisive — otherwise the same five people take everything and nobody new is ever hired.

The rule that governs all of it

If we cannot say why, we do not show it. Every shortlist carries its reasoning to the buyer. Every worker can see why he was not shortlisted and what would change that. A ranking that decides who earns this week must be answerable to the people it ranks.

What the machine is for, and what it is not for

It does this

  • Turns a spoken description into a structured profile or job specification
  • Understands meaning across languages and informal trade vocabulary
  • Narrows hundreds of people to three, with reasons
  • Notices duplicate accounts, recycled photographs and false claims
  • Drafts the record entry from the job's own evidence, for the worker to approve

It never does this

  • Choose who is hired
  • Remove someone from the platform
  • Decide a disagreement between two people
  • Set a price with no band and no explanation
  • Rank anyone by anything it cannot put into words
12 · Conditions on the ground

Designed for the phone in his hand

Every one of these is a design input, not an excuse. A product that assumes a good phone, a strong signal and constant power is a product for people who already have options.

An inexpensive Android

Small screen, little storage, an old browser. A progressive web app, no install required, everything reachable with one thumb.

A small data bundle

Light mode by default: text first, images on request, photographs compressed on the device before they are sent. Data spent is data the user chose to spend.

Power that comes and goes

Work continues offline. Check-ins, photographs and quotes queue locally and send when the signal returns, with the pending count always visible.

Three languages

isiNdebele, chiShona and English throughout, including notifications, receipts and disputes. Trade vocabulary follows local usage rather than translation.

Mixed reading confidence

Speech accepted everywhere it matters. Icons always paired with words. Numbers, times and money written the way people say them.

WhatsApp is home

For many workers it is the internet. The full working cycle runs there, so the app is an upgrade rather than a barrier.

14 · Build sequence

Three stages, each ending in a decision

Stages end when they are proven, not when the calendar says so. If a stage does not reach its mark we repair it rather than adding scope on top of a weak foundation.

STAGE ONE

Make one trade work in a few suburbs

  • Web app and WhatsApp channel
  • Plumbing and electrical only
  • Two or three suburbs
  • Identity, record, deposit, sign-off, disagreements
  • Crew orders filled by hand, by a person
  • Supply recruited through crew leads and the polytechnic
MOVE ON WHEN

Fill rate above 85% and buyers returning above 35%, held for eight straight weeks.

STAGE TWO

Widen the city, build the crew product

  • The remaining four trades
  • All of Bulawayo
  • Crew Hire console, built from what the manual work taught us
  • Crew lead team accounts
  • Grades and rate bands live
  • Domestic and care work, as recurring placement
MOVE ON WHEN

Three organisations ordering crews repeatedly, and each Quick Hire job paying for itself.

STAGE THREE

Make the record valuable elsewhere

  • A second city
  • Shareable verified records for outside employers
  • Tool finance and insurance written against standing
  • Training institutions feeding verified graduates in
  • Native apps once reliable alerts justify them
PROVEN WHEN

An outside party — a lender, an insurer, an employer — acts on a Maricho record. That is the whole idea, confirmed.
