# Maricho Business Case

## Financial Modeling & Revenue Streams
Maricho is built to monetize the demand side (the buyer) while remaining entirely free for the supply side (the worker). Charging the worker selects for desperation, not skill.

- **Quick Hire (Households/Small Businesses):** A percentage of the job, charged to the buyer and disclosed before booking. This fee buys the verification, the held money, and the redo guarantee.
- **Crew Hire (Organizations):** A placement fee or a monthly subscription, invoiced to the organization. The value proposition is fill rate and attendance records.
- **Holding Funds:** Funds in Zimbabwe will likely run on a licensed rail (mobile money merchant arrangement like EcoCash or InnBucks or a bank partner) rather than Maricho holding balances directly.

## Cost & Resource Constraints (Self-Hosted/Environmental)
- **User Devices:** Must support older Androids with low storage. App must be a Progressive Web App (PWA) requiring no install, accessible via a single thumb.
- **Data Usage:** Light mode by default (text first, images on request). Photos compressed on-device before sending.
- **Connectivity:** Offline-first architecture. Check-ins, photos, and quotes queue locally and send when signal returns.
- **Primary Channel:** Deep integration with WhatsApp. The full working cycle can run on WhatsApp for those who never open the PWA.
- **Languages:** isiNdebele, chiShona, and English throughout. High reliance on voice inputs and iconography for mixed reading confidence.

## Build Sequence & ROI Timelines (Phased Rollout)
Stages end when proven, not by the calendar.

### Stage One: Make one trade work in a few suburbs
- **Scope:** Web app and WhatsApp channel. Plumbing and electrical only. 2-3 suburbs. Hand-filled crew orders.
- **Move on when:** Fill rate is above 85% and buyers return at above 35%, held for eight straight weeks.

### Stage Two: Widen the city, build the crew product
- **Scope:** Remaining 4 trades (Solar, Welding, Building, Appliance repair). All of Bulawayo. Crew Hire console for organizations. Crew lead team accounts. Grades and rate bands live. Domestic and care work as recurring placements.
- **Move on when:** Three organizations order crews repeatedly, and each Quick Hire job pays for itself.

### Stage Three: Make the record valuable elsewhere
- **Scope:** A second city. Shareable verified records for outside employers. Tool finance and insurance written against worker standing. Native apps once reliable alerts justify them.
- **Proven when:** An outside party (lender, insurer, employer) acts on a Maricho record.
