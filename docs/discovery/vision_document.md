# Maricho Vision Document

## Product Vision
"Skilled work you can see, hire, and prove." 
Maricho turns informal trade work into something that leaves a trace — allowing households to hire with confidence, employers to staff sites quickly, and tradesmen to own a permanent record of their work.

## Target Audience and Market
- **Pilot Market:** Bulawayo, Zimbabwe.
- **Buyers:**
  - **Quick Hire:** Households and small businesses needing one-off jobs (e.g., homeowners with leaking geysers).
  - **Crew Hire:** Organizations needing to staff crews (e.g., operations managers needing bricklayers).
- **Workers:** Skilled informal tradesmen (Plumbers, Electricians, Solar installers, Welders, Builders, Appliance repairers) and recurring domestic/care workers.
- **Environmental Constraints:** Users have inexpensive Androids, small data bundles, and intermittent power/signal. WhatsApp is the primary internet channel.

## Core Problems Solved
1. **Skill is invisible:** Tradesmen lack verifiable certificates. *Solution: Issue evidence (photos, dates, client sign-off) and an earned grade.*
2. **Trust is unverifiable:** Buyers can't distinguish good tradesmen from bad until the job is half done. *Solution: Verify identity, publish standing, inspect before quoting, and guarantee a redo.*
3. **Payment is unenforceable:** Tradesmen are vulnerable to unpaid labor. *Solution: Hold a deposit from booking and release the balance upon sign-off.*

## Success Parameters (Target by Month Six)
- **Fill rate (Requests that put a worker at the door):** Above 85%
- **Time to accept (Request to a worker saying yes):** Under 30 minutes for urgent trades
- **Buyers who come back (Second booking within 90 days):** Above 35%
- **Workers earning properly (Active workers with 4+ paid jobs/month):** Above 40%
- **Spread of earnings (Share of payouts reaching busiest 10%):** Below 40%
- **Jobs put on hold (Disagreements as a share of jobs):** Below 6%
- **Worker responses upheld:** Tracked (near zero means the process is decorative)
- **Records shared (Workers sending record outside Maricho):** Tracked
